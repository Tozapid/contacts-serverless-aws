An address book of your own

Contacts is a private, invitation-only address book. Each user keeps their own contacts with every field of Apple Contacts and Google Contacts, the full history of changes of each card, groups, family relations, duplicate search and merge, and import and export of vCard files.

The address book syncs with the phones and computers of its owner: with iPhone, iPad, Mac, Thunderbird, Android and Outlook over CardDAV, with Google Contacts and with iCloud.

The source code: github.com/Tozapid/contacts-serverless-aws.
